A few care basics

Start with the conditions.

Small checks help you choose a plant that suits your space. Care needs differ by plant, season and growing conditions, so use these points as a starting place.

01

Notice the light

Check whether the room gets direct sunlight, bright indirect light or shade. Match your choice to the space you have.

02

Check before watering

Feel the soil before adding water. Pot size, temperature and season affect how quickly it dries.

03

Let excess water drain

Choose a pot with drainage and a suitable potting mix. Avoid leaving the roots sitting in standing water.
