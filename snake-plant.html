Snake plant

Dracaena trifasciata
KSh 250 · listed priceSize: small (approx. 20–30cm)Pot: 12cm plastic

Upright, architectural leaves. Allow the soil to dry between waterings and use a free-draining potting mix. Tolerant of low light but grows faster in bright, indirect light.

Care at a glance

Light

Bright, indirect to low light. Avoid harsh direct sun on young leaves.

Water

Water sparingly — let the top 3–4cm dry between waterings. Reduce watering in cool months.

Soil & pot

Free-draining mix, pot with drainage. Add perlite for aeration.

Propagation & tips

  • Propagate by division — separate clumps when repotting in spring.
  • Wipe leaves occasionally to remove dust and check for pests.
  • Yellowing lower leaves usually mean overwatering; cut back and let soil dry.
